The WRX STi
The Impreza WRX STi (and its recent models, simply named WRX and WRX STi) are notable in tuner and enthusiast circles thanks to its rally-inspired performance and distinctive boxer engine. The WRX STi, with its AWD system, turbocharged engine, aggressive design and high-performance capabilities, is an iconic tuner car with rally heritage.

Boxer Engine
Central to Subaru's identity in the tuner scene is the horizontally opposed "boxer" engine. This engine configuration, known for its low center of gravity and unique power delivery, is a key part in Subaru's performance cars. The boxer engine within the WRX and STi feature in models like the WRX and WRX STi. Within the WRX and its STi variant, it is turbocharged and is famous for the strength of its boost and acceleration.

The BRZ
On the other end of the spectrum, the Subaru BRZ, developed in collaboration with Toyota (also sold as the Toyota 86 and Scion FR-S), has gained popularity for its lightweight design and nimble handling. While the BRZ features a different driving philosophy compared to the WRX STi, its rear-wheel-drive layout and focus on balance make it a favorite among enthusiasts looking for a more accessible and drift-friendly platform.

Subaru's Rally History
Subaru's involvement in rallying, especially with the Impreza, is well documented The iconic blue and gold livery of the Subaru World Rally Team became synonymous with success in the World Rally Competition (WRC), contributing to its credibility and fame among performance enthusiasts. It notably shares this reputation with the Mitsubishi Lancer Evolution, a comparable rally monster and famous tuner car.

Pop Culture and Tuner Fame
Subaru's presence in car culture and Hollywood has been notably highlighted in various films and video games, further positioning itself in the tuner world. The STi, in particular, has been featured in movies and games, amplifying its appeal to a broad audience of car enthusiasts. Notably, it appears in Baby Driver and Initial D.

Subaru's impact on tuner culture is characterized by the rally performance of the WRX STi, the unique turbocharged boxer engine of the WRX and the WRX STi, and lightweight and easily tunable design of the BRZ. From rally competitions to the road, Subaru has developed a reputation for their powerful, growling cars.

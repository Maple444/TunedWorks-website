The RX-7 The Mazda RX-7 is one of the most iconic cars in tuning history thanks to its sleek design and revolutionary rotary engine. The RX-7, particularly the third-generation FD model as well as the previous generation FC model, has captured the hearts of enthusiasts with its lightweight chassis, balanced handling, and the iconic sound of the rotary engine. Rotary Engine Central to Mazda's tuner legacy is the rotary (or wankel) engine, a unique engine known for its high-revving nature and smooth power delivery. It is primarily found in Mazda’s performance cars from this era (e.g. the RX-7, RX-8, and that one race car). The rotary engine's compact size and high power-to-weight ratio have made it a favorite among enthusiasts seeking unconventional performance. In models like the RX-7 and RX-8, the rotary engine became synonymous with Mazda's commitment to innovation and driving excitement. Miata/MX-5 Roadster While the RX-7 represents Mazda's high-performance aspirations, the Mazda Miata (MX-5) is its little, more affordable sibling. The Miata, known for its balanced handling and open-top driving experience, has become a staple in the tuner scene. Its lightweight chassis and responsive dynamics make it a popular choice for modification and track-day enthusiasts. Miatas are known for being affordable and extremely versatile, allowing for modifications ranging from simple engine upgrades to engine swaps and aero kits. Mazdaspeed Hatches Mazda's performance division, Mazdaspeed, has played a significant role in the brand's tuner reputation. Models like the Mazdaspeed3 and Mazdaspeed6, family cars equipped with turbocharged engines and enhanced performance capabilities, were catered to enthusiasts seeking a mix of everyday practicality and spirited driving dynamics. These models are affordable and practical despite their performance under the hood. Pop Culture and Tuner Fame Mazda's influence in tuner culture extends beyond the roads, with appearances in popular media and racing games. The RX-7, in particular, has been featured in movies, video games, and various forms of motorsport, elevating its status among enthusiasts. Two notable examples are Han’s RX-7 from the Fast and Furious movies and the Takahashi brothers in Initial D.


Mazda's presence in tuner culture is shown by the iconic RX-7 and its rotary engine, the accessible and versatile Miata, and the turbocharged performance offerings from Mazdaspeed.

The GT-R and Skyline model line
The Nissan GT-R, affectionately known as "Godzilla," has earned a great reputation in the tuner community for its performance and advanced technology such as the ATTESA AWD system that prevents slipping even at high load. From the R32 to the latest R35 model, the GT-R has remained one of the most famous cars in the tuner community and pop culture due to its relentless acceleration and precision handling.

Beyond the GT-R, Nissan's Skyline range boasted a lineup of performance-oriented models such as the more accessible GTS-T. The Skyline's heritage in motorsport and tuning culture has contributed to its high status among enthusiasts, with models like the R32 and R34 GT-R gaining a cult following worldwide.


The Z Line
Nissan's Z line, represented by models like the 240Z, 300ZX, 350z, and 370Z, holds a special place in the hearts of enthusiasts worldwide. The Z cars are known for their sleek designs and high performance while being relatively affordable. With a legacy spanning several generations, the Z line remains a cornerstone of Nissan's tuner heritage.


The Silvia Line
The Nissan Silvia, particularly the S13, S14, and S15 generations, has become central within the car tuning and drifting community. It is particularly loved by enthusiasts for its rear-wheel-drive layout, responsive chassis, and abundant aftermarket support, the Silvia has cemented its status as a drift icon. Its timeless design and tunable nature make it a favorite among those seeking sideways thrills.


240sx and 180sx
Closely related to the Silvia (based on the S platform), the Nissan 240SX and 180SX have also made significant impacts in the tuning world. The 240SX, with its KA24DE engine in the U.S. market, and the 180SX, featuring the turbocharged CA18DET and later the SR20DET engines in Japan, offer versatile platforms for modification. These models are particularly popular in the drifting community due to their rear-wheel-drive configuration and strong aftermarket support.


Nissan in Pop and Tuner Culture
Nissan's influence in tuner culture extends beyond the garage, with appearances in movies, video games, and motorsport events. The GT-R is especially known for its famous appearances in the 2 Fast 2 Furious as well as as Initial D and the 350z likewise has become famous for drifting, now enjoying widespread use in drifting events around the world.

The GT-R
The Nissan GT-R, affectionately known as "Godzilla," has earned a great reputation in the tuner community for its performance and advanced technology such as the ATTESA AWD system that prevents slipping even at high load. From the R32 to the latest R35 model, the GT-R has remained one of the most famous cars in the tuner community and pop culture due to its relentless acceleration and precision handling.


The Z
Nissan's Z line, represented by models like the 240Z, 300ZX, 350z, and 370Z, holds a special place in the hearts of enthusiasts worldwide. The Z cars are known for their sleek designs and high performance while being relatively affordable. With a legacy spanning several generations, the Z line remains a cornerstone of Nissan's tuner heritage.

The Silvia Line
The Nissan Silvia, particularly the S13, S14, and S15 generations, has become synonymous with the art of drifting. It is particularly loved by enthusiasts for its rear-wheel-drive layout, responsive chassis, and abundant aftermarket support, the Silvia has cemented its status as a drift icon. Its timeless design and tunable nature make it a favorite among those seeking sideways thrills.

Most Iconic: The Skyline
Beyond the GT-R, Nissan's Skyline range boasts a diverse lineup of performance-oriented models, including the iconic GT-R variants and the more accessible GTS-T. The Skyline's heritage in motorsport and tuning culture has contributed to its high status among enthusiasts, with models like the R34 GT-R gaining a cult following worldwide.

Nissan in Pop and Tuner Culture
Nissan's influence in tuner culture extends beyond the garage, with appearances in movies, video games, and motorsport events. The GT-R is especially known for its famous appearances in the 2 Fast 2 Furious as well as as Initial D, and the 350z likewise has become famous for drifting (also its Fast and Furious Tokyo Drift appearance)

Nissan, in the tuner community, is defined by iconic models like the GT-R, Z, Silvia, and Skyline, each representing a performance, innovation, and driving excitement. Given their legacy in motorsports with cars such as the R380, they have remained one of the defining brands of tuning culture.
